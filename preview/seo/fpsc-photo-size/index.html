FPSC Photo Size for the Online Form

Photo mode opens at 30 KB, the figure in the FPSC Section Officers guideline.

The 30 KB photograph line

The Federal Public Service Commission guideline for the Section Officers Promotional Examination tells applicants to upload a passport-size photograph with the Browse button, and it says the picture must be less than 30 KB in JPEG only. That PDF is on the FPSC site: SOPE online guideline. This page selects 30 KB so you can make a file in that range.

The document is from that examination’s instructions, not a promise about every later consolidated post. Read the advertisement you are using, and the current online guideline, before you submit. This tool does not file the application, pay the fee, or check your eligibility.

Drop the photo and press Compress. The JPEG is built on this device, metadata from the camera file is not copied across, and the name matches the size you see, such as photo-28.6kb.jpg. If you also need a signature, the advert usually prints that limit separately. Open signature resize and set the number it shows.

A CNIC scan and a fee challan are different uploads. Do not assume they share the 30 KB photo rule.

Questions

Is 30 KB the limit on every FPSC post today?

The cited guideline says less than 30 KB for that photograph step. Check the latest advert, because a later post can change it.

Does the tool crop to passport shape?

Only if you enter both width and height under More options. Otherwise it keeps the photo’s own shape and controls the file size.

Where is the PPSC figure?

PPSC is a different commission. Its working target on this site is 25 KB, on the PPSC photo page.
